description: The weighbridge check before the first truck
---

Run `tare-due`, `overloads`, `weighbridges`, `consent` and `customers` with `--json`. Report in this order: trucks that need a re-weigh before they load, any overload in the last 7 days and its carrier, bridges overdue for a test, sites over 90% of consent, customers on credit hold. Keep it to one screen. Never change a record.
